====================================================================================
WHAT THE EXPERIMENT MUST ACHIEVE
====================================================================================
gamma_SC = 3.34887,  discriminating signal |F0| = 0.042086

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. REQUIRED PRECISION ON THE OFFSET
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   power    sd(F) required   vs benchmark median
    0.50           0.02559                1.11x
    0.80           0.01693                1.68x
    0.90           0.01438                1.98x
    0.95           0.01279                2.23x
  benchmark median sd(F) at rho = 0 is 0.02849
  so reaching 80% power needs a 1.68-fold reduction in sd,
  i.e. about 2.8x more replicates at fixed per-assay precision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE SHARED TRITIUM REFERENCE REDUCES THE VARIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
     rho   sqrt(1+g^2-2 g rho)    required s (%)   gain vs rho=0
    0.00                3.4950             0.484            1.00x
    0.30                3.1946             0.530            1.09x
    0.50                2.9776             0.568            1.17x
    0.70                2.7434             0.617            1.27x
    0.90                2.4874             0.680            1.41x
    0.99                2.3631             0.716            1.48x
  benchmark median relative precision: K_HT 0.81%, K_DT 0.69%

  Common-mode decomposition, s_L^2 = s_ref^2 + s_L,ind^2:
    shared reference enters Var(F) with weight (gamma-1)^2 = 5.517
    independent errors enter with weight 1 + gamma^2 = 12.215
    ratio 2.21, the factor bought by measuring
    H/T and D/T in one triple-label mixture rather than separately.

  At fixed s_D the variance is minimized at s_H = gamma rho s_D, where
  Var(F) = gamma^2 s_D^2 (1 - rho^2): the reference error cancels
  exactly in the limit rho -> 1. Reporting the covariance would let a
  reader exploit this; none of the sources do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE COMMITMENT COSTS MORE THAN THE PRECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  F_obs - F_min over 73 records: median 0.0000, max 0.3962
  in units of |F0|: median 0.0, max 9.4
  52 records have zero gap (L_H >= gamma, so the
  endpoint is the commitment-free value and nothing is lost).
  Where the gap is nonzero it dwarfs the signal, so suppressing the
  commitment matters more than improving precision.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW WELL AN INDEPENDENT COMMITMENT MEASUREMENT MUST BE KNOWN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Knowing c point identifies F, with dF/dc from Proposition 2.
  Requiring the induced sd(F) to stay below half the signal:
   K_HT   K_DT  c assumed    |dF/dc|   max sd(c)/c
   5.04   1.65       8.08     0.0875          3.0%
   5.04   1.65      40.40     0.0014         37.3%
   6.44   1.92      10.88     0.0635          3.0%
   6.44   1.92      54.40     0.0010         39.3%
   2.36   1.49       2.72     0.0971          8.0%
   2.36   1.49      13.60     0.0010        149.7%
  A commitment known to a few tens of percent already contributes less
  than the KIE precision does, so pre-steady-state or isotope-trapping
  determination of c is the cheaper route to identification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TEMPERATURES DO NOT SUBSTITUTE FOR PRECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The series statistic is a maximum over temperatures and does not
  average down: adding temperatures raises the selection bias rather
  than shrinking the standard error. Pooling across temperatures needs
  F constant in T, which is part of what is under test. Under that
  assumption sd falls as 1/sqrt(n_T):
   n_T    sd(F) pooled    power at rho=0
     1         0.02849             0.434
     3         0.01645             0.820
     5         0.01274             0.951
    10         0.00901             0.999
    20         0.00637             1.000
  Reaching 80% power by temperatures alone needs 3 of them at present precision.
