2. HOW BADLY WOULD THE ERRORS HAVE TO BE UNDERSTATED?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  to stop excluding the gated envelope F0   : errors x 2.60
  to stop excluding the semiclassical locus : errors x 1.96
  Errors on the average (0.07, 0.02) match the standard
  error of the 3 determinations (0.067, 0.018), so these
  factors are the margin as it stands, with no allowance for the 'SD' label.

3. POOLING THE THREE INDEPENDENT DETERMINATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  individual offsets: +0.1509, +0.1689, +0.0851
  inverse-variance mean +0.1337 +- 0.0349, 95% LCB +0.0763
  heterogeneity Q = 1.11 on 2 d.o.f., p = 0.575
  published average row gives +0.1287; the two agree to 0.0050
  No detectable heterogeneity, so the determinations are mutually
  consistent and the published average is not carrying the result.
