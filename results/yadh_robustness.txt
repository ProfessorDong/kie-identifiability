========================================================================================
HOW HARD IS THE YEAST ADH CONCLUSION TO BREAK?
========================================================================================

1. MASS CONVENTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
convention        gamma_SC         F0     F_obs       sd   95% LCB   > F0   > 0
reduced C-H        3.34887  -0.042086    0.1287   0.0399    0.0630    YES   YES
bare atomic        3.26281  -0.055321    0.1759   0.0390    0.1118    YES   YES
integer 1:2:3      3.25725  -0.055522    0.1789   0.0389    0.1149    YES   YES
  Cha et al. themselves used 3.26. The conclusion does not depend on
  which convention is adopted, and is strongest under theirs.

2. HOW BADLY WOULD THE ERRORS HAVE TO BE UNDERSTATED?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  to stop excluding the gated envelope F0   : errors x 2.60
  to stop excluding the semiclassical locus : errors x 1.96
  The published errors are standard deviations of >=5 determinations.
  Treated as standard errors of the mean they would be smaller by about
  sqrt(5) = 2.24, so the margin above is conservative already.

3. POOLING THE THREE INDEPENDENT DETERMINATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  individual offsets: +0.1509, +0.1689, +0.0851
  inverse-variance mean +0.1337 +- 0.0349, 95% LCB +0.0763
  heterogeneity Q = 1.11 on 2 d.o.f., p = 0.575
  published average row gives +0.1287; the two agree to 0.0050
  No detectable heterogeneity, so the determinations are mutually
  consistent and the published average is not carrying the result.

4. AGAINST THE SUMMED VIBRONIC ENVELOPE OVER A BOUNDED BOX
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  yeast ADH 95% lower bound: +0.0630
      lambda range   max B_vib   excluded?
              5-60      0.6096          no
             10-60      0.2956          no
             15-60      0.0061         YES
             20-60     -0.0002         YES
  Reorganization energies below ~10 kcal/mol are not plausible for
  enzymatic hydride transfer; above that the observation excludes the
  summed model as well as the ground-channel one.

5. REVERSIBILITY AND COMMITMENT, RESTATED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  L_H = 8.40 > gamma_SC, so the set is the open
  half-line (+0.1287, inf): no commitment lowers the endpoint.
  vacuity window: EMPTY (F_obs > 0), so no
  equilibrium isotope effect makes the observation uninformative.
