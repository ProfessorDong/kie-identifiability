3. WHAT A DECISIVE SINGLE MEASUREMENT WOULD REQUIRE
   One observation excludes the ridge iff F_obs > F0 with the minimum
   at c -> infinity, i.e. a commitment-free measurement with
   ln K_HT - gamma_SC ln K_DT > -0.0421.

     K_DT^obs   K_HT^obs required   observed exponent
         1.50               3.728              3.2451
         2.00               9.769              3.2881
         2.50              20.624              3.3029
         3.00              37.978              3.3106

   The required exponent sits just below gamma_SC in every case, so the
   experiment must be essentially commitment-free.  That is the single
   most informative thing this analysis says about experimental design:
   suppressing the commitment matters more than precision or
   temperature coverage, because the commitment is what makes the
   identifiable set a half-line open in the wrong direction.
