Proposition 2 is mechanism-free and applies unchanged. Only the
reference exponent changes, and it is contested: 3.26-3.34 (Bahnson
et al.) versus ~4.8 (Kohen and Jensen). Exclusion holds iff the
reference lies below the observed exponent, and is then robust to
commitment by the asymmetry theorem.

  L_H > gamma_obs in 10 of 10 records, as the theorem requires.

  robust against a 3.34 reference: 8 of 10

  robust across the WHOLE disputed range: YADH wild type, LADH L57F, LADH F93W

  These three exclude the semiclassical locus whichever side of the
  Kohen-Jensen dispute is taken, and by the asymmetry theorem the
  exclusion cannot be an artifact of kinetic complexity. They are the
  strongest surviving evidence in the primary literature for a
  breakdown of mass scaling in enzymatic hydrogen transfer.

  The remaining records are not evidence against tunneling. Their
  identified sets contain zero and every positive value, so they are
  simply uninformative at a 4.8 reference.
