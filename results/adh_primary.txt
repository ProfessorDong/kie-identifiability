        10 of 11 sets contain 0 and every positive value:
        consistent with tunneling, with the semiclassical locus, and

        contradictory. The other 1 lies above 0.
  YADH: offset +0.1287, excludes both.

  Bahnson et al. (1993) give the reason: the LADH reaction is partially
  rate limited by benzaldehyde dissociation, whereas YADH has a rate-
  limiting chemical step. Commitment drives the observed offset down,
  which is the sign of every LADH value.

  The framework therefore predicts which systems can inform before any
  mechanism is assumed: those whose chemical step is rate limiting.
