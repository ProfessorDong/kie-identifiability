CONCLUSION
====================================================================================
Across every series the model can actually describe, the 95% interval
on the discriminating invariant is at least 4.8 times the
separation between the two mechanisms. Not one series discriminates.

The limitation is not the number of enzymes: four families, five
systems, three organisms and two chemical steps all give the same
answer. It is that F must be recovered through the commitment map,
whose masking displaces the observed offset by up to 20x the signal.

Precision target for a decisive experiment: a 95% interval on F
inside 0.042 log-KIE units, i.e. at least 5-fold tighter than the best series here.
